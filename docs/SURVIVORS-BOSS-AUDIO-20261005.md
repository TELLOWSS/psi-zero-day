# Objective Patrol and Equipment Mix

## Implemented
- Elapsed time is not victory. The map's designated boss, authored control zones and intervention quota must be resolved, then the existing interruptible handoff must finish.
- No minimum patrol waiting gate. Completing preliminary controls exposes the boss after 15 seconds; 60 seconds is the fallback arrival, not a completion timer.
- Existing map-specific cart, suspended load, rubble and gas boss families are mandatory. Advanced workface names now identify the dangerous entity as well as the location. Gas bosses use the existing telegraphed pressure-pulse cycle rather than continuous contact alone.
- Live objective prioritizes an active boss's real name and health. Selection and result copy no longer promise a 180-second automatic clear.
- Score envelope rises from 0.55 to 0.95 (about +4.7 dB). Event music ducking rises from 0.35 to 0.65; dialogue ducking from 0.24 to 0.38. Saved volume and mute preferences remain untouched; session limiter and bounded voices remain active.
- Non-worker synthesized launch/impact gain increases from 0.13/0.20 to 0.32/0.42. Equipment pulse, hiss, arc, plastic and low-frequency signatures differ. Metal, rubble and gas have separate impact tails. Worker confirmations remain identical and quiet.
- Existing supplied recordings remain in use. Satellite/hunter playback variants distinguish evolved equipment from base radio/drone without stacking duplicate voices. Procedural sound is not claimed as an orchestral final recording.

## Verification
- Full automated engine/UI suite; typecheck and production build.
- Browser fixtures at 1440x900, 390x844 and 844x390: actual map boss, continued play after 180 seconds, nonblank canvas, no document overflow or page errors; ten finite non-silent equipment PCM signatures.
- Controlled browser fixtures are not natural human playthroughs. Opt-in normal-input agent calibration covers 50 stages with three seeds each: 140 objective victories and 10 defeats; each stage has at least one objective victory. Original twenty-stage cases finish 59/60. Stage 16 seed 42 and nine advanced cases fail: these remain balance-review evidence, not a claimed universal completion baseline or human win rate.

## Director Review
- Listen on phone speaker, headphones and desktop speakers before final sound approval. Increase does not prove perceptual quality or integrated loudness compliance.
- Boss families currently reuse finished industrial art and authored behavior. Fifty individually drawn bosses and cinematic phase-transition assets are not delivered here.
- Future boss art should embody each workface's dangerous process, never turn workers into attack targets. Review warning readability and natural late-stage completion times before adding second-phase attacks.
