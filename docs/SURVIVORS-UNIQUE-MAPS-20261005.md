# Fifty Independent Stage Backgrounds

## Corrected Direction

The previous fifty-stage expansion reused eighteen paintings. That does not meet the Director's request for independent stage maps. Thirty-two repeated assignments are replaced with thirty-two newly generated native raster paintings. The first assignment of each of the eighteen original paintings remains; no background URI is assigned to two stages.

These are new paintings, not hue variants, renamed copies, mirrored images or crop aliases. Each has an individually authored workface brief: material, service equipment, perimeter landmark, floor joint pattern and flat circulation marking. The brief registry is `content/design/survivors-unique-maps-v1.json`; the selected native source and exact runtime asset registry is `content/design/survivors-unique-map-assets-v1.json`.

Runtime imports only the lean URI assignments in `content/design/survivors-stage-backgrounds-v1.json`, not prompts or local source paths.

## Production Scope

32 built-in imagegen originals at 1536x1024; WebP quality 90, 17,539,976 bytes total. Original generated PNGs retained. No franchise art, stock download, placeholder or geometric replacement. Retained originals keep existing provenance.

Strict overhead framing, construction materials visible, no people/UI/enemies baked into floors. Central play surface stays continuous. The retained 18 maps and these 32 new maps use the existing world and collision contract; scenery is not new collision geometry. This corrects background reuse, not an expansion to fifty bespoke terrain/collision/AI systems. Do not claim otherwise.

Selected-stage preview, stage thumbnails and combat ground use the same stage-to-art registry. Floor references are limited to three textures, preserving the current selection and live combat background. This limits held references, not the browser's independent resource cache; no claim of measured physical-device RAM or FPS.

- Existing construction equipment and hazards still render as live entities.
