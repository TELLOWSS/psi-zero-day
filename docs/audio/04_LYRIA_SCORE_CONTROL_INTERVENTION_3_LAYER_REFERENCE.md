## INSTRUMENTATION
Primary:
- short strings
- restrained French horn
- restrained low brass
- timpani / low orchestral transient

Optional:

Forbidden:
- choir
- vocals
- synth drop
- trailer braam
- giant bass drop

## SHAPE
- 0.00–0.20 sec: clear orchestral attack
- 0.20–0.90 sec: authority / body
- 0.90–2.50 sec: controlled rapid decay
- after 2.5–3.0 sec: near silence or clean tail

## CRITICAL MIX RULE
동시에 다음 Foley가 재생된다.
- heavy truck pneumatic air brake
- construction radio squelch
- access-control barrier clack

따라서:
- orchestral attack은 분명하지만 0.9초 이후 빠르게 자리를 비운다.
- air brake transient를 덮지 않는다.
- radio upper-mid를 덮지 않는다.
- barrier clack을 giant orchestral hit로 가리지 않는다.
