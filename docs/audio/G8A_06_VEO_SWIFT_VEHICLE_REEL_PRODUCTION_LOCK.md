## Purpose
Generate one **8-second audio-first video** for extracting four clean, separated construction-truck Foley candidates:
1. `swift.engine_loop`
2. `swift.reverse_alarm`
3. `swift.gravel_tire`

No music. No dialogue. No narration. No readable text, logo, brand or manufacturer identity.

## Visual setup
A realistic large apartment-construction worksite vehicle area. One heavy dump truck. Fixed camera. No people. No cinematic camera movement. Visuals exist only to ground realistic audio generation.

## Strict 8-second timeline
- **0.00–1.70** — isolated heavy construction dump-truck diesel idle/slight load. No horn, reverse alarm or brake hiss.
- **1.70–2.00** — near silence.
- **2.00–3.50** — isolated industrial reverse warning alarm, phone-speaker readable, engine nearly absent.
- **3.50–3.80** — near silence.
- **3.80–5.30** — slow heavy tire crushing coarse gravel, believable several-ton mass, no alarm.
- **5.30–5.70** — near silence.
- **5.70–7.40** — realistic pneumatic heavy-truck air-brake release/stop, clean attack and natural mechanical tail.
- **7.40–8.00** — natural decay / near silence.

## Quality
Real construction scale, clean transients, no clipping, no trailer processing, no monster/sci-fi treatment, no exaggerated sub-bass. Extraction separation is more important than cinematic beauty.

## Reject
Reject music, narration, spoken words, sports-car engine character, overlapping regions, reverse alarm hidden under engine, cinematic impact replacing the air brake, or continuous background noise that prevents clean extraction.

## Final instruction
Generate the media directly. Do not explain it. The returned MP4 will be given back to ChatGPT unedited for extraction and QA.
